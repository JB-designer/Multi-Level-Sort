# Multi-Level Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Multi-Level Sort works only on a page where you click its icon. It reads the item cards on that page (titles, links, thumbnail addresses and numbers such as views or ratings) to sort them. If you ask it to read more pages, it loads further pages of that list, found through the site's own page-number links. It loads them either in the background with your normal login for that site, or in one background tab that it closes when done.

## Stored on your device only

Everything is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer:

- your last 10 sorted results (page address, page title and card details);
- your sort settings for each site address;
- the panel's position for each site address;
- your light/dark theme choice.

These stay until you click "Reset site setup" in the panel (sort settings for that site) or remove the extension (everything).

## Network

No server, analytics, tracking or remote code. The extension connects only to the website you are sorting and, to show thumbnails, to the image addresses that website uses. Like any web page, those servers see your IP address.

## Sharing

We do not collect, sell, share or transfer any data.
